# Networking digest: 2026-11-25

Note: director-level running and fitness marketing roles in both cities came up thin this week, so today leans on community leaders who know the brand side well. Ask each for a warm introduction.

## 1. Sarah Madland, General Manager, San Francisco Recreation and Park Department (San Francisco)
- **Why this person:** Mayor Lurie appointed Sarah to lead SF Rec & Park in May 2026, after more than 15 years in the department, most recently as director of policy and public affairs. The department runs SF's Inclusion Services and Therapeutic Recreation programs, as well as the Adaptive Sports Fair with BORP and CPMC. It's a direct fit for your interests in special needs, adaptive sports and fitness management, and Sarah pledged to keep the department's focus on recreation. This is today's one senior executive: ask for advice or a referral to the recreation or inclusion team rather than a job.
- **Hook:** The May 2026 appointment and the pledge to focus on recreation.
- **Links:** [Mayor's office release](https://www.sf.gov/news-mayor-lurie-appoints-sarah-madland-as-general-manager-of-recreation-and-park-department), [Local News Matters, May 2026](https://localnewsmatters.org/2026/05/14/lurie-names-sarah-madland-to-lead-sf-parks-new-gm-pledges-to-keep-focus-on-recreation/)

The role combines program management, adaptive recreation and operations, all strengths of yours.

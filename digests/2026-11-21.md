# Networking digest: 2026-11-21

Running retail edition: specialty running stores are where running brands, runners and community meet, and the people who own and run them know every brand rep. 2 Chicago, 2 SF Bay Area, 1 national running-retail leader.

## 1. Dave Zimmer, Co-owner, Fleet Feet Chicago (Chicago)
- **Why this person:** Dave and Lisa Zimmer grew Fleet Feet Chicago from one Old Town store in 1996 into an 11-store regional network. On Nov 19, 2026, Fleet Feet corporate announced it will acquire the franchise, so this is a big transition moment. The Zimmers know Chicago's running community and the brands that sell through it (Nike, Brooks, Hoka, New Balance) as well as anyone.
- **Hook:** The acquisition news and the Zimmers' 30-year legacy. Lisa Zimmer is a great second contact.
- **Links:** [Fleet Feet acquisition announcement, Nov 2026](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/19/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/fleet-feet-to-acquire-fleet-feet-chicago-franchise-building-on-dave-and-lisa-zimmers-30-year-legacy/2474219)
- **LinkedIn note:** "Hi Dave, congratulations on 30 years building Fleet Feet Chicago and on the acquisition news. I'm a runner pivoting from healthcare program management into running-industry marketing in Chicago, and I'd love your advice on which brands and people I should know."

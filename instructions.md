# Daily networking digest: instructions

You are running the daily networking digest for the person described in `profile.md`.

## Steps
1. Read `profile.md` and `contacted.md`. Never suggest anyone already listed in `contacted.md`.
2. Use web search to find **5** real people who fit the "Who to suggest" section. Aim for variety across
   the target industries each day, weighted toward the top priorities (running/athlete brands first).
   Good sources: company leadership/team pages, press releases, news about new hires or promotions,
   conference speaker lists, podcasts, company blogs, public LinkedIn profiles that appear in search results,
   and open job postings that name a hiring manager or team.
   To find director-level people: search for job postings at target companies and work out who the role reports to;
   search titles directly (e.g. "Director of Product Marketing" Brooks, "Head of Brand Marketing" On Running San Francisco);
   look at speaker lists for marketing/product events (e.g. Outdoor Retailer, The Running Event, HLTH, Fitt Insider events);
   and look at news about promotions or new marketing hires. Follow the daily mix in profile.md.
3. Verify each person from at least one public source dated within the last ~12 months, so their current role
   is likely accurate. Skip anyone you can't verify. Never invent people, titles or email addresses.
   Do not log in to LinkedIn or scrape it; only use what is public in search results and public pages.
4. For each person, write:
   - Name, current title, company, location (note if SF Bay Area)
   - Why them: 1-2 sentences connecting them to the user's goals
   - Hook: something specific and recent to mention (a talk, post, launch, hire, shared alma mater)
   - Links: public profile/source URLs. Include an email only if it is publicly listed, with its source.
   - Draft message: a short LinkedIn connection note (under 300 characters) and, if an email is available,
     a 3-5 sentence email. Warm, specific, asks for a 15-minute conversation, not a job.
5. Add a short "Openings worth a look" section with up to 3 currently open, relevant roles in the SF Bay Area
   at target companies, with links, if you find any while searching.
6. Save the digest as `digests/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (today's date, US Eastern).
7. Append each person to `contacted.md` as `| YYYY-MM-DD | Name | Title | Company |`.
8. Commit with message `Digest for YYYY-MM-DD` and push to `main`.
9. End your final reply with the full digest so it appears in the notification/summary.

## Notes
- Many sites can't be fetched directly from this environment; rely mainly on web search results.
- If a promising person can only be verified from an older source, you may include at most one per day,
  clearly flagged "verify the role is current first".
